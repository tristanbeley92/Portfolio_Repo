INSIDE THE BUILD01 The starting point02 What I built03 Under the hood04 What stayed with me
01

The starting point

The team set out to make nutrition guidance easier for students to explore, with meal suggestions that account for a limited budget. The time constraint: build and present the idea in 36 hours.

02

What I built

I co-developed the React and TypeScript interface and helped integrate multimodal AI into the experience. The project uses Google Gemini 2.5 Flash and DigitalOcean Gradient AI, with Tailwind CSS for the interface.

03

Under the hood

The prototype takes photo input and generates personalized meal suggestions. It is a hackathon exploration, not a validated diagnostic tool; the useful engineering challenge was connecting image input, model responses, and a readable result.

04

What stayed with me

NutriScan won the Sun Life Best Health Hack and Best Use of DigitalOcean AI awards at HackWestern 12. The sprint pushed the team to coordinate interface work and AI integration around a working demo.
